How it works
  • Your browser generates a random 256-bit key and puts it in the link after #. Browsers never send that part to the server.
  • The secret is encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM and relayed live over a WebSocket. Nothing is stored.
  • The receiver decrypts locally and sends back a signed confirmation.
  • The link works once, for 5 minutes. Anyone holding the full link can read the secret — share it over a channel you trust.
